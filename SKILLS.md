## Format
`{language} [{version}] (since {learnDate},{activeDuration},{usageLevel},{casual})`  
- `{learnDate}` = Year / Date when i have learned _it_  
- `{activeDuration}` = How long i used _it_  
- `{usageLevel}` = How often i still use it.
  - Verry High = I use it Almost Always.
  - High = At least 5 Hours a Week. (&gt;10% of a Workday)
  - Medium = At least 5 Hours in a Month. (&gt;~5% of a Workday)
  - Low = Maybe up to 48 Hours a Year. (&lt;0.5%)
- `{casual}` = Means, that i used _it_ mainly for Personal Purposes.
